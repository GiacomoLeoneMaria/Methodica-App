## 2. Description of the App

Methodica is a personal productivity application designed to help individuals organize their time and priorities. It includes a calendar with events and reminders, a Kanban board for tasks, a Priority Square to balance the areas of your life, a focus timer, and simple finance tracking (income, expenses, and recurring transactions). The current version of the App stores all data exclusively on the user's device and does not require an internet connection or account registration to use its core features.
