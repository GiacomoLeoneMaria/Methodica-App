## 2. Summary

**As of the date of this policy, Methodica does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with the Developer or any third party.**

All data you enter in the App (events, tasks, priority areas, finance entries, custom icons, settings) is stored exclusively on your device and is not sent to any server, cloud service, or third party by the current version of the App.

This may change in the future — for example, if optional account creation, cloud backup/sync, analytics, or advertising are introduced. Any such change will only apply going forward, will be clearly disclosed, and this policy will be updated *before* the change takes effect (see Sections 8 and 14).

## 3. Data We Do NOT Currently Collect

The current version of Methodica does not collect:

- Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, IP address)
- Location data

- Usage analytics or crash reports

## 4. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data **exclusively on your device**:

| Data | Purpose | Where stored |
|---|---|---|
| Events (title, date, time, reminders, notes, icon, color) | Core app functionality (calendar) | Local storage (AsyncStorage) |
| Tasks, subtasks, tags and Kanban status | Core app functionality (Kanban) | Local storage (AsyncStorage) |
| Priority areas and their weights | Core app functionality (Priority Square) | Local storage (AsyncStorage) |
| Finance entries (transactions and recurring transactions) | Core app functionality (Finance) | Local storage (AsyncStorage) |
| Custom icon images you choose | Personalizing events, tasks and areas | Device file system (referenced locally) |
| Theme, language and notification preferences | User preferences | Local storage (AsyncStorage) |

This data is entirely under your control. You can export, import, or permanently delete it at any time from within the App.

## 5. Device Permissions

Methodica may request the following device permissions:

### Notifications

- **Purpose:** To deliver reminders for your events and daily reminders for your active tasks
- **When:** When you enable notifications or add a reminder
- **How it works:** Reminders are **local notifications**, created and delivered by your device. We do not operate a notification server, we do not use remote push notification tokens, and we never receive the content of your reminders
- **Shared:** Never

On Android, the App may also use the exact alarm permission so that reminders fire at the time you chose.

### Camera & Photo Library

- **Purpose:** To let you use a photo as a custom icon for an event, task, or area
- **When:** Only when you explicitly choose to add an image
- **Stored:** Locally on your device only
- **Shared:** Never — images are never uploaded or transmitted by the current version of the App

### Files & Storage

- **Purpose:** To let you export a backup file and import it back, using your device's file picker and share sheet (on Android, storage access may be required by the operating system)
- **Shared:** Only where *you* choose to send the file. Once you share a backup to a service such as email or a cloud drive, that service's own privacy policy applies

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device settings. The App will continue to function without them, with reduced functionality for the related feature.

### If you are in Canada or Brazil (PIPEDA / LGPD), or elsewhere

## 12. Data Security and Retention

Since all data is currently stored locally on your device, security depends on your device's own protections (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend:

- Keeping your device OS up to date
- Using a screen lock
- Making regular backups using the App's "Export Data" feature

The Developer has no access to your data and cannot recover it if your device is lost or reset. If cloud storage is introduced in the future, this section will be updated to describe the additional safeguards in place for data in transit and at rest.

You control how long your data is kept. Data persists on your device until you:

- Manually delete events, tasks, areas, or finance entries within the App
- Use the "Clear All Data" function in the App
- Uninstall the App (which removes all local data)

## 13. Backups and Files You Share

The "Export Data" feature creates a JSON file containing your data. This file is created on your device and leaves it only if *you* choose to save or send it (for example to a cloud drive, by email, or to another device). The Developer never receives it. Treat backup files as you would any personal document: anyone who obtains the file can read its contents. When you import a backup, the App shows a summary of what it contains and asks for confirmation before replacing your current data.
